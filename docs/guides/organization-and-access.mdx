---
title: "Organizations and access"
description: "Understand access to information and your account permissions."
---

Your Comply access belongs to an Organization. Its administrator manages available accounts and permissions. View or change only information you are authorized to use.

## Request or change access

Contact your Organization administrator when you need an account, change roles or no longer need access.

## Protect your account

- Use a unique password and never share it.
- Complete multi-factor authentication when prompted.
- Sign out on shared devices.
- Report activity you do not recognize to your Organization administrator.

Your available access depends on the permissions assigned to your account.
