title: "Get started with Comply"
description: "Sign in and find the main work areas."
---

Open [trycomply.com](https://trycomply.com) to use Comply. An Organization administrator creates your access. Public sign-up is not available.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get access">
    Ask your Organization administrator to create your account and share the sign-in link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Enter your email and password. Set up or verify multi-factor authentication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    See metrics and pending alerts in the overview. Open work areas from the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Can't sign in?

- Check that you used the email linked to your account.
- If your session expires, sign in again and complete multi-factor verification.
- If your credentials or multi-factor access do not work, contact your Organization administrator. Never send your password or verification codes in a message.
