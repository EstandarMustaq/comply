Open [trycomplyz.com](https://trycomplyz.com) to use Complyz. An Organization administrator creates your access. Public sign-up is not available.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get access">
    Ask your Organization administrator to create your account and share the sign-in link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Enter the email and password you received. Follow the prompts to set up or verify
    multi-factor authentication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    After you sign in, the overview shows key metrics and pending alerts. Use the sidebar
    to open customer profiles, imports, reports and settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Can't sign in?

- Check that you used the email linked to your account.
- If your session expires, sign in again and complete multi-factor verification.
- If your credentials or multi-factor access do not work, contact your Organization administrator. Never send your password or verification codes in a message.
