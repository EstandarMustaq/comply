---
title: "Review and submit reports"
description: "Review reports, complete drafts and download exports."
---

Open **Reports** to view available records. Some reports can only be changed while they are drafts. You may not be able to edit a report after submission.

## Review a draft

1. Open the report and check its details.
2. Fill in missing fields with verified information.
3. Review the values and reference ID before you submit.
4. Submit only when you are authorized and the report is ready.

Download available exports from the report actions. Protect exported files and share them only with authorized people.
