---
title: "Manage customer profiles"
description: "View, search and create customer profiles in Complyz."
---

Open **Clients** to view the profiles available in your Organization. Search by name or identifier, then open a profile to review its details.

## Create a profile

1. Select **Add customer**.
2. Enter the required name and identity details.
3. Add available information, such as the NUIT and customer type.
4. Review the details and save the profile.

Check identity details before you save. If you cannot create or update a profile, ask your Organization administrator for help.

## Review risk information

The profile shows the information currently on record. Update only details you have verified and are authorized to change. Important changes appear in the activity history.
