Open **Alerts** to see records that need review. The overview also highlights priority alerts.

## Review an alert

1. Open the alert and review the customer and related activity.
2. Compare the available data with the customer's profile.
3. Add a clear review note and choose the next status available to your role.
4. Save the change and confirm the status has updated.

If you do not have enough information or permission to finish the review, leave the alert in the right status and follow your Organization's process. Record conclusions only when the data supports them.
