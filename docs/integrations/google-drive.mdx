**Status: planned. Not available yet.**

A future integration may let you select Google Drive files for authorized Comply workflows. Its scope, requested permissions, supported formats and sync behavior are not defined as available features.

Do not share Google credentials with Comply or automate uploads based on this page. We will publish requirements when the integration is implemented and ready to use.
