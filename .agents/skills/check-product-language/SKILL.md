# Check product language

1. Read `docs/standards/product-language.md` and use its voice and vocabulary as the source of truth.
2. Identify the audience, current state, and next action. Write short, active English that leads with the action or state and names the relevant boundary.
3. Keep API, event, and storage identifiers stable and separate from display labels. Use `Organization` and `CommercialClient` only for their defined product concepts.
4. Keep product claims within verified behavior. Keep internal acronyms and implementation details, unsupported legal claims, citations, and deadlines out of customer-facing copy.
5. Review every changed loading, empty, success, error, and denied state for accuracy, accessibility, and a clear next step. Check the surrounding workflow, not only the edited string.
6. Preserve stable wire values and immutable historical migrations. Write engineering instructions and internal comments in concise technical English.
